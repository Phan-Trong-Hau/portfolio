Why I built it

I just wanted to play pool with my friends. Every pool app I tried was laggy, buggy, full of ads, or didn’t let us play together.

So I built my own to fix my own pain point. I vibe coded it with AI: I described what I wanted, played it, found what felt wrong, and kept iterating until it felt like a real game.

What it does

Play with friends

Create a room, send the 4-digit code or link, and you’re playing. No account, no install. Reload the page and the match continues.

Play against the bot

Three levels: Easy, Medium, Hard. The bot picks shots, places the cue ball after fouls, and sometimes teases you with emotes.

Built for phones

Touch controls for aiming, fine-tuning, power, and cue tip position (follow, draw, side spin). Mouse and keyboard work too.

Install as an app

A PWA that opens full screen in landscape and still lets you play the bot offline.

Make it yours

Name, avatar (upload a photo or pick one), 4 table colors, 6 cues with a 3D preview.

Real match feel

Turn timer from 15 seconds to 5 minutes, match history, emotes, and real recorded ball sounds.

How it works

  • 3D renderingThree.js draws the table, balls, and cue, with a live 3D preview for cue and table picks.
  • Billiards physicsHand-written physics with sub-stepped simulation per frame, ball–ball and ball–cushion collisions, and top, back, and side spin.
  • Real-time multiplayerTwo devices sync through a public MQTT broker over secure WebSockets. Room state is kept on the broker so players can reload and rejoin. Only the player whose turn it is can shoot; re-racking needs both players to agree.
  • Bot AIShot search using ghost-ball aiming, cut angles, and clear-path checks, tuned into three difficulty levels.
  • PWA & offlineWeb app manifest and a service worker that always fetches the latest version, with an offline fallback.
  • AudioCC0 recordings converted to mono 16-bit, trimmed, and embedded as base64 so the game needs no extra downloads.
  • DeliveryOne static HTML file on GitHub Pages. No backend, no build step, no ads.
