Built at Mysheo. The product belongs to the company; students sign in with their own accounts.

About

Mysheo runs online English classes. Study is where students, teachers, and staff manage everything around those classes.

Besides building core features, I coordinated tasks, reviewed code, mentored new engineers, and handled production deployments.

What it does

Core LMS

Student enrollment, attendance tracking, assignment management, and teacher scheduling.

Roles

Role-based administration for students, teachers, and staff.

Real-time & sync

WebSocket communication and real-time sync with Google Sheets.

AI and media

OpenAI API features and media storage on Cloudflare.

How it works

  • FrontendReact/Next.js in TypeScript with Material UI.
  • BackendExpress.js with MariaDB and Redis.
  • QualityCypress end-to-end tests and Jest unit tests; Docker for deployments.
