Lofi Panel

A tiny lofi and ambient radio that lives in the Linux top panel, with a dancing pixel cat and 15 curated 24/7 stations.

  • Python
  • GTK 3
  • GStreamer
  • D-Bus
  • Linux
Source code ↗

Why I built it

I like coding with lofi on, but a browser tab for music is easy to lose and heavy to keep open.

Lofi Panel puts the radio right in the top bar: one click to play, scroll to change station, and a cat that dances along.

What it does

Panel mascot

An animated cat bobs to the beat and falls asleep when paused.

Mini player

Click the cat for big controls, a live equalizer, and a station picker.

15 stations

Lofi hip hop, rainy café, city pop, jazz, solo piano, V-Pop, and more.

Own volume

Separate volume that leaves the system volume alone; auto-starts on login.

How it works

  • StreamingGStreamer playbin streams in memory; nothing is downloaded to disk.
  • Panel iconA StatusNotifierItem over D-Bus, working on COSMIC, GNOME, and other FreeDesktop panels.
  • UIA GTK 3 popover window built with PyGObject; animated SVG frames for the cat and waves.
  • CLIlofi-panel ui, toggle, next, stop, and status commands.
