About

Each enrollment campaign needed its own landing page: course details, an FAQ, and a clear path to sign up.

Our team of three built and shipped these pages end to end, from the first commit to production.

What it does

Campaign pages

Course details, schedules, and the teacher’s story, written to convert visitors into students.

FAQ

A questions section that answers the common doubts before sign-up.

Responsive

Works on phones and desktops alike.
