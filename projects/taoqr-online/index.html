About

A simple, fast QR tool with no sign-up. Pick what the code should hold, style it, add a logo, and download it.

QR codes are generated in the browser, so things like Wi-Fi passwords never leave your device.

What it does

Many QR types

Link, text, Wi-Fi, phone, email, SMS, vCard business card, and VietQR bank transfer.

Design it

Colors and an optional brand logo in the middle.

Export

PNG or SVG at 500 to 2000 px, or copy the image directly.

Scan QR codes

Scan with the phone’s rear camera or from an uploaded image.

How it works

  • Next.js 16 + React 19One route per QR type, with its own metadata for search.
  • Generationqr-code-styling renders styled codes with logos on the client.
  • ScanninggetUserMedia opens the rear camera; jsQR decodes frames and uploaded images.
  • SEO & PWASitemap, robots rules, web app manifest, and a blog.
